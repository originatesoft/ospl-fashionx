Purpose-built ecommerce platform for fashion and apparel brands — developed by Originate Soft Pvt Ltd

**OSPL Fashionx** is a purpose-built ecommerce platform for fashion and apparel brands that need more than a generic online store. Developed by **Originate Soft Pvt Ltd**, it brings together everything a clothing business requires to sell confidently online — product catalog management, size-variant handling, order fulfilment, returns, and customer engagement — in one clean, scalable system designed specifically for the way fashion retail works.

---

## Why OSPL Fashionx

Fashion retail is not like selling electronics or groceries. Collections change seasonally. Products come in ten sizes, six colours, and three fits. Customers expect high-resolution imagery, lookbook-style browsing, and smooth checkout across both mobile and desktop. Off-the-shelf ecommerce tools either buckle under this complexity or strip it down to the point of being useless.

OSPL Fashionx was built by **[ready-made ecommerce software for fashion platform]( https://originatesoft.com/ecommerce-software-solution/fashion-apparel-software/)** to close that gap — purpose-engineered for apparel businesses, not retrofitted from a horizontal ecommerce template.

## Core Features

### Product & Catalog Management
- Multi-variant product listings with size, colour, and fit matrix support
- SKU-level inventory tracking per variant
- Bulk product import and catalogue upload
- Season and collection tagging for organised browsing
- Rich media support — multiple images per SKU, zoom, and video

### Storefront & Shopping Experience
- Mobile-first, responsive storefront with customisable layouts
- Lookbook and editorial-style product display pages
- Advanced filters: category, size, colour, price range, brand, and occasion
- Wishlist, recently viewed, and save-for-later functionality
- Real-time stock availability indicators per variant

### Order & Fulfilment Management
- Centralised order dashboard with full status tracking
- Automated order confirmation and shipment notification emails
- Partial fulfilment support for multi-item orders
- Return and exchange workflow with configurable policies
- Shipping integrations with major logistics providers

### Customer Accounts & Loyalty
- Registered customer profiles with order history
- Address book management for faster repeat checkout
- Loyalty points and rewards engine
- Coupon and discount code management (flat, percentage, category-specific)
- Referral programme support

### Payments & Checkout
- Multi-gateway payment integration (cards, UPI, wallets, net banking, COD)
- Guest checkout and one-page checkout options
- Saved card and wallet top-up support
- GST-compliant invoice generation
- Currency and language localisation for cross-border selling

### Admin & Business Intelligence
- Seller dashboard with real-time sales, revenue, and inventory reports
- Low-stock and out-of-stock alerts
- Customer behaviour analytics (top products, cart abandonment, session data)
- Promotions calendar and flash sale scheduler
- Role-based admin access for team members

### Marketing & Growth Tools
- SEO-ready product and category pages with editable meta fields
- Email marketing integration hooks
- Abandoned cart recovery automation
- Social media sharing and product feed export (Google Shopping, Meta)
- Blog and editorial content module for fashion storytelling
